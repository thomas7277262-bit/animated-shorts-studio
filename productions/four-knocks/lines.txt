In my town, there's only one rule. Never open the door after midnight.
Not even if it's your mom's voice calling your name.
Every house has three locks. Every window has bars. And at twelve o'clock, the whole town turns off its lights.
My name is Ben. And I'm the kid who counts knocks.
Every night, I lie in bed and listen. Knock. Knock. Knock.
And I write every single one in my notebook.
At school, they call me Knock Boy. They bang on my desk and laugh.
But Grandma never laughed.
Forty years ago, her little brother opened the door after midnight. They never found him.
One night, she held my hand and whispered the secret.
"Real people knock three times, Ben. It always knocks four."
I didn't believe her. Until last Friday.
Mom and Dad went out for dinner. I was home alone.
At eleven fifty-nine, I locked all three locks. The whole house went silent.
Then, at exactly midnight… knock.
"Ben? Sweetie, it's Mom. I forgot my keys."
It was her voice. Her exact voice.
I reached for the lock. Then I remembered Grandma. And I started counting.
One. Two. Three.
Four.
My hand froze on the handle.
"Ben, open the door. It's so cold out here."
I backed away slowly. The knocking got louder. Four. Four. Four.
Then my phone buzzed. It was Mom.
"Hi sweetie, we're staying at Grandma's tonight. Is everything okay?"
I looked back at the door. The knocking had stopped.
The house was completely silent.
And then, I heard it again. Knock. Knock. Knock. Knock.
But this time… it was coming from inside my closet.
