Every Saturday, the Maple Street shelter held an adoption day. And every Saturday, one little dog watched from the very last cage. His name was Patch. One floppy ear, a patchy brown coat… and only three legs. Families walked in smiling. They looked at Patch… and kept on walking. "Too scruffy," said one dad. "Too slow," said a grandma. One by one, every other dog went home. Patch stayed. Ms. Reyes always whispered, "Your family's coming, buddy." But week after week… nobody came. Nobody, except Owen. Owen knew exactly how Patch felt. He was always the last kid picked. Every Saturday, he sat by the last cage and told Patch about his week. Patch wagged so hard his whole body wiggled. For the first time, they both had a best friend. But Owen's mom said they couldn't afford a dog. Then one Saturday, a shiny white car pulled up. The Vandermeers, the richest family in town. Matching white clothes, giant sunglasses, and a list for the perfect puppy. Mrs. Vandermeer lowered her sunglasses at Patch and laughed. "Ew. That one's broken." Patch's tail stopped wagging. They chose a fluffy white puppy named Duchess and drove away. That night, Patch didn't touch his food. Then the storm came. Thunder shook Maple Street, and the power went out. At the Vandermeer mansion, the wind blew the golden gate wide open. And Duchess slipped right through it. The tiny puppy was swept into the flooded street, toward a storm drain. She clung to the grate, crying, but nobody could hear her. Nobody… except Patch! Ms. Reyes was checking the kennels when his ears shot up, and Patch bolted out the door. Three legs. Full speed. Straight into the storm! He splashed through the freezing water, grabbed Duchess by the collar, and pulled. He dragged her onto the sidewalk. Then he barked and barked, until every porch light flicked on. By morning, the whole town knew his name. Mrs. Vandermeer knelt down with tears in her eyes. "I called you broken," she whispered. "You were the bravest one of all." The Vandermeers paid for every adoption at the shelter that day. Then Ms. Reyes smiled. "Patch, somebody's here for you." It was Owen, holding a brand new red collar. Patch hopped into his arms. For the first time ever, he was going home. Because sometimes the one nobody picks… is the one everybody needs.
