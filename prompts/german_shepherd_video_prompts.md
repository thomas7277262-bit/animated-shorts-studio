# German Shepherd Versions: HD Realistic Video Prompts (30s+)

These are the same four viral video ideas, rewritten so every pet is a **German Shepherd**. They follow the same structure as the earlier prompts: style, characters, setting, timed shots, camera/style details and sound. Shots marked **[NEW]** are the added scenes that take each video past 30 seconds.

**How to use:** Most AI video tools (Higgsfield, Kling, Veo, Runway, Sora) generate 5–10 seconds per clip. Use the full prompt as the master description, generate each timed **SHOT** as its own clip with the same dog description pasted in, then join them in CapCut. Reusing one reference image of the dog for every shot keeps the dog looking the same throughout.

## PROMPT 1: "Not Today. Wrong Dog." (Grim Reaper vs. German Shepherd Owner)

> Cinematic, ultra-realistic 4K film-look short, 9:16 or 1:1, shot like a moody Hollywood action thriller with anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, warm tungsten interior light against cold blue night exterior, subtle film grain, slow deliberate camera moves. Total length about 34 seconds.
>
> CHARACTERS: The Grim Reaper, tall and towering in a heavy, ragged, pitch-black hooded robe, with a realistic bone-white skull face, hollow eye sockets, and long skeletal fingers gripping a tall wooden scythe with a curved, rusted steel blade. The Homeowner, an original character: a lean, quiet man in his late 40s with shoulder-length dark brown hair, a short salt-and-pepper beard, tired but dangerous eyes, a small healed scar on his cheekbone, wearing a fitted black button-up shirt and dark trousers. The Puppy, a 10-week-old German Shepherd puppy with a classic black-and-tan saddle coat, black mask on the muzzle, fluffy cheeks, one ear standing up and one ear still floppy, oversized paws, a teal collar, and big, innocent dark-brown eyes, playing with a small colorful rubber ball.
>
> SETTING: A white clapboard two-story suburban house at night, warm brass porch lanterns glowing, red brick front steps, neat green lawn, dark trees. Inside: a cozy, dimly lit living room with a deep red Persian rug, a brown leather sofa, bookshelves, and a warm floor lamp.
>
> SHOT 1 (0–3s): Over-the-shoulder from inside the doorway. The front door swings open to reveal the Grim Reaper on the porch, scythe in hand, bony fingers slowly lifting. A chilly mist drifts behind him.
> SHOT 2 (3–5s): Medium shot of the Homeowner inside the warm hallway, staring back calmly, expressionless.
> SHOT 3 (5–7s): Slow push-in on the Reaper's skull face under the hood, shadows sliding across the bone.
> SHOT 4 (7–10s): Over the Reaper's shoulder: his skeletal hand raises and points one long finger past the Homeowner, deeper into the house.
> SHOT 5 (10–14s): Low, ground-level shot on the red rug. The German Shepherd puppy pounces on its ball, tail wagging, then sits and tilts its head toward the door, floppy ear flipping over, with innocent curious eyes.
> SHOT 6 (14–17s): Extreme close-up of the Homeowner's face. His jaw tightens, his eyes go cold and narrow. Total silence. A single low bass rumble.
> SHOT 7 (17–20s): Wide exterior of the house from the lawn. Beat of stillness, then a loud crash: the Reaper is thrown backward out of the front door, flying through the air and landing flat on the grass, scythe clattering beside him, robe sprawled.
> SHOT 8 (20–25s): The Homeowner steps onto the porch carrying a pump-action shotgun lowered at his side, walks slowly down the brick steps, and stands over the dazed Reaper without a word.
> SHOT 9 [NEW] (25–28s): The German Shepherd puppy trots out onto the porch behind him, stands at the top step with its chest puffed out, and lets out one tiny, squeaky "woof" at the Reaper. The Reaper slowly raises a bony finger as if to argue, then lowers it in defeat.
> SHOT 10 (28–31s): The Homeowner scoops the puppy up under one arm, walks back inside, and closes the door. The porch lanterns flicker.
> SHOT 11 [NEW] (31–34s): Interior, warm light. The Homeowner sits on the leather sofa; the puppy climbs onto his chest and licks his chin. He gives a tiny half-smile and scratches behind its ears. Text on screen: "Not today. Wrong dog."
>
> CAMERA & STYLE DETAILS: Keep the puppy shots low and soft-lit so the black-and-tan fur glows warmly, a contrast to the cold blue exterior. Keep the Reaper's robe moving slowly in the night breeze.
>
> SOUND: Deep cinematic drones, a creaking door, crash and thud on the lawn, the puppy's squeaky bark. No dialogue. Photorealistic textures, natural motion, accurate German Shepherd puppy anatomy, no cartoon look, no distorted hands, no extra fingers or legs.

## PROMPT 2: "He Said NO to the Dog Bed… So His German Shepherd Made Sure He HAD to Buy It"

> Ultra-realistic, handheld smartphone-style vertical video, 9:16, 1080x1920, natural bright fluorescent store lighting, true-to-life colors, casual documentary feel like a real viral TikTok filmed by a friend, slight natural camera shake, crisp HD detail on fur and fabric. Total length about 30 seconds.
>
> CHARACTERS: A 4-month-old German Shepherd puppy, gangly and clumsy, with a black-and-tan saddle coat, black mask, huge upright ears that look too big for its head, long legs, oversized paws, a black collar, and very expressive amber-brown eyes, dramatic and stubborn. Its owner, a big, broad-shouldered man in his early 30s with a thick full black beard, short dark hair with a fade, tan skin, wearing a plain black t-shirt and black athletic shorts, playful and easygoing.
>
> SETTING: The pet-bed aisle of a large, bright retail superstore. Tall gray metal shelves packed with round plush dog beds in gray, beige faux-fur, and navy blue. Shiny white tiled floor, bright ceiling lights, yellow price tags on the shelf edges. On the lowest shelf sits one extra-large round gray bolstered dog bed.
>
> SHOT 1 (0–2s): Wide shot from behind, the man strolling down the aisle with the German Shepherd puppy trotting beside him on a loose leash, ears bouncing.
> SHOT 2 (2–4s): The puppy stops, stares at the big gray bed, and hops straight into it, paws sliding on the plush fabric.
> SHOT 3 (4–6s): Close-up: the puppy circles twice, flops down in the center with a big sigh, and looks up at the camera with a satisfied "this is mine" face.
> SHOT 4 [NEW] (6–9s): The man crouches into frame, shakes his head with a grin, and wags his finger: "No, we're not buying that." The puppy tilts its head, ears swiveling, unimpressed.

> SHOT 6 (12–15s): He sets it down at the edge; the puppy instantly scrambles back to the center and lies flat.
> SHOT 7 (15–19s): The man leans in close. The puppy sits up, throws its head back, and lets out loud, dramatic German Shepherd "talking": a mix of grumbles, woo-woos and howls right at him, ears pinned forward, total teenage-dog outrage.

> SHOT 9 (22–25s): The man laughs so hard he covers his face with his hand, shoulders shaking.
> SHOT 10 [NEW] (25–28s): Cut to the checkout lane. The man carries the big gray bed in his arms with the puppy sitting proudly inside it like royalty, head high, while he shakes his head with a defeated smile.
> SHOT 11 [NEW] (28–30s): Final close-up of the puppy's smug face looking into the camera, one ear flicking, then a slow blink. Text overlay: "He said NO… he said YES."
>
> CAMERA & STYLE DETAILS: The camera stays at shelf height, close to the dog, so the puppy's big ears and drama fill the frame. Keep ceiling-light reflections on the glossy floor and the plush texture of the bed sharp. The dog's acting is the star: head tilts, swiveling ears, and wide, offended eyes.
>
> SOUND: Store ambience, sneakers on tile, the puppy's grumbles and howls, the man's deep laughter. Photorealistic, natural animal behavior, accurate German Shepherd anatomy, no warped limbs, no extra legs, no text glitches.

## PROMPT 3: "Sorry for Fat-Shaming You" (Sulking German Shepherd Puppy)

> Ultra-realistic, cozy handheld smartphone vertical video, 9:16, soft natural daylight from a nearby window, warm neutral tones, shallow focus on the puppy, gentle and intimate home-video feel, HD fur detail. Caption bar at the top on a black background in white sans-serif text: "His feelings are hurt cause I said he got fat 🥲". Total length about 30 seconds.
>
> CHARACTER: A chubby, fluffy long-coat German Shepherd puppy, about 10–12 weeks old, with a round soft belly, plush black-and-tan fur, a dark saddle on its back, tan legs, fluffy "pantaloons" on its back legs, a bushy tail, and soft ears that are only half standing. Only the owner's arm and hand appear: a slim woman's forearm, light skin, short natural nails, no jewelry.
>
> SETTING: A big comfy light-gray linen sofa with oversized rumpled throw pillows and a soft cream knit blanket. Calm, minimal, Scandinavian living room.
>
> SHOT 1 (0–3s): From slightly behind and to the side, the puppy sits with its back to the camera, face pressed deep into the corner between two gray pillows, completely hiding its head, round fluffy body hunched and motionless. The picture of a sulk.
> SHOT 2 (3–6s): The camera slowly moves around to the side. The puppy pushes its face even deeper into the pillow, ears flattening back.
> SHOT 3 (6–10s): The owner's hand enters and softly strokes the top of its head and back. The puppy doesn't move; it stays buried and ignores her.
> SHOT 4 (10–14s): Her hand gently pats its fluffy side and tries to lift its chin. The puppy shifts away and sinks further into the pillows with a tiny huff, sliding one big paw over its face.
> SHOT 5 (14–17s): Close-up of the round fluffy belly and pantaloons as she pets it. The puppy lets out a long, dramatic German Shepherd groan, body deflating.
> SHOT 6 [NEW] (17–21s): Her hand returns holding a small treat beside the pillow. One amber eye peeks out at the treat, then hides again: still offended, but tempted.
> SHOT 7 [NEW] (21–25s): She places the treat down and keeps stroking. The puppy slowly pulls its face out, eats the treat, then looks up at the camera with big, sad, guilt-trip eyes, ears drooping sideways.
> SHOT 8 [NEW] (25–30s): The owner scoops the puppy into a hug; it melts against her chest and rests its head on her shoulder, bushy tail giving a slow wag. Caption changes to: "Okay okay… you're just fluffy 🤍".
>
> CAMERA & STYLE DETAILS: Keep the camera at sofa-cushion height, close to the puppy, with slow, gentle handheld drift and no fast cuts until the hug. Light should feel like a quiet late morning: soft, diffused, no harsh shadows, with a warm glow on the black-and-tan coat so the long fluffy strands catch the light. The puppy's body language sells the emotion: head down, ears flat, shoulders hunched, completely still except for slow breathing and the occasional dramatic sigh. Keep the background softly blurred in beige and gray so the dark puppy stands out.
>
> SOUND: Quiet room tone, soft fabric rustles, the puppy's sighs and huffs, a gentle woman's laugh off-camera saying "I'm sorry, baby." Photorealistic, natural puppy movement, accurate anatomy, no extra paws, no warped hands, no distorted text.

## PROMPT 4: "Mom Is Calling for Immediate Attention for the Puppy"

> Ultra-realistic, static fixed-camera vertical video, 9:16, 1080x1920, like a real home security or phone camera propped on the floor, soft warm evening indoor lamp light, beige walls, authentic home-video look, HD detail on fur. Total length about 30 seconds.

A mother German Shepherd: an adult female with a rich black-and-tan saddle coat, black mask, tall upright ears, intelligent amber eyes, and a calm but bossy expression. Two tiny German Shepherd puppies, about 7 weeks old, fluffy and round, mostly dark with tan legs, soft floppy ears, and stubby tails.
>
> SETTING: A plain bedroom. A low black wooden slatted platform bed frame with a quilted white-and-gray mattress, gray pillow, and a white-and-gray checkered blanket. Light beige carpet, a white door and dark doorway in the background. A small round bright blue plastic pet bowl, empty, on the carpet beside the bed.
>
> SHOT 1 (0–3s): The man is fast asleep. One puppy waddles out from under the bed frame dragging the empty blue bowl in its mouth.
> SHOT 2 (3–7s): The puppy drops the bowl, sits beside it, looks up at the sleeping man, and lets out tiny high-pitched yips. Then it paws the bowl so it clatters and spins on the carpet.
> SHOT 3 (7–10s): The puppy flops onto its back, rolls and wiggles next to the bowl, little legs kicking, in a dramatic tiny tantrum.
> SHOT 4 (10–13s): The mother German Shepherd walks in through the doorway with the second puppy toddling behind her. Both puppies sit by the empty bowl looking up at her.
> SHOT 5 (13–15s): The mother sniffs the empty bowl, looks at the bed, and steps up, placing both front paws on the mattress.
> SHOT 6 (15–17s): She pushes her long nose right into the man's face and nudges his cheek firmly, then gives one sharp, low "woof."
> SHOT 7 (17–20s): The man jolts awake, lifts his head, and stares at her in surprise. She holds his gaze, then turns her head down toward the bowl as if pointing.
> SHOT 8 [NEW] (20–23s): He leans over the edge, sees both puppies sitting beside the empty blue bowl staring up at him, and rubs his face with a sleepy, defeated laugh.
> SHOT 9 [NEW] (23–27s): He gets up in his red shirt, returns with a bag of puppy food, and kneels to fill the bowl. The puppies rush in, tails wagging, eating noisily.

>

>
> SOUND: Quiet bedroom ambience, the plastic bowl scraping on carpet, tiny puppy yips, the mother's low, insistent woof, the man's sleepy groan and laugh, kibble pouring into the bowl. Photorealistic, natural dog movement and anatomy, correct number of legs and tails, no morphing between dogs, no distorted faces.
