# Seedance 2.5: "Aaj Nahi. Galat Kutta." (Daytime, 30s)

Two 15-second generations, joined in CapCut. Use the same reference image of the puppy and the owner for both parts if your Seedance interface supports image references.

## PART 1 (0–15s)

Ultra-realistic cinematic short film, 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 24fps, shot on an ARRI Alexa with anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, natural film grain. Bright sunny afternoon in a quiet Indian suburban neighborhood: clear blue sky, strong golden sunlight, crisp shadows, green trees swaying gently, birds chirping. The house is a clean white two-story bungalow with a small wooden front door, a red brick porch with potted plants and a neat green lawn with a low white gate.

Characters (keep consistent in every shot):
- THE ANGEL OF DEATH: about 7 feet tall, in a heavy, ragged, pitch-black hooded robe that looks completely out of place in the bright sunshine. A realistic bone-white skull face with empty dark eye sockets, long skeletal fingers, holding a tall wooden scythe with a rusted curved steel blade. Thin wisps of black smoke drift off the robe even in daylight. He casts a long dark shadow on the sunny porch. Deep, slow, echoing voice.
- THE OWNER: an original character, not resembling any real actor. A calm, serious Indian man in his late 30s with medium-length messy black hair, a short neat beard, sharp cold eyes and a small scar near his left eyebrow. He wears a fitted plain black t-shirt, dark jeans and slippers, and holds a steel cup of chai.
- THE PUPPY: a 10-week-old German Shepherd puppy with a black-and-tan saddle coat, black mask on the muzzle, fluffy cheeks, one ear standing up and one ear floppy, oversized paws, a teal collar and big, shiny, innocent brown eyes.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Wide shot from the lawn. The Angel of Death walks slowly up the sunny garden path, robe dragging over the grass, birds suddenly going silent. He stops at the front door and knocks three times with a bony knuckle. Sound: three heavy, hollow knocks.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Medium shot from inside the doorway. The Owner opens the door, sipping his chai, squinting into the bright sunlight, completely unbothered by the skeleton in front of him. He says flatly in Hindi: "कौन हो तुम?" (Who are you?)

Shot 3 (8–12s): Slow push-in on the skull face under the hood, sunlight glinting on the bone, smoke curling around it. The Angel of Death answers in a deep, echoing voice: "मौत का फ़रिश्ता… आज मुझे किसी को साथ ले जाना है।" (The Angel of Death… today I must take someone with me.)

Shot 4 (12–15s): Close-up of the Owner taking another slow sip of chai, eyes narrowing slightly. He asks: "किसको?" (Who?) The Angel slowly raises one long skeletal finger and points past him into the house.

Style: photorealistic, natural human and animal motion, realistic sunlight and shadows, cinematic color grade with warm highlights and teal shadows. Funny contrast between the dark, spooky Angel of Death and the cheerful sunny day. No cartoon look, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no text, no watermark.

## PART 2 (15–30s)

Continue the exact same scene, characters and bright sunny afternoon lighting as Part 1. Ultra-realistic cinematic short film, 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 24fps, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain, golden sunlight, crisp shadows.

Characters (same as before):
- THE ANGEL OF DEATH: a tall figure in a ragged pitch-black hooded robe with a bone-white skull face, skeletal fingers and a rusted scythe, with black smoke drifting off him in the sunlight.
- THE OWNER: an original character, a calm, serious Indian man in his late 30s with messy black hair, a short beard, cold eyes, a small scar near his left eyebrow, a black t-shirt and dark jeans, holding a steel cup of chai.
- THE PUPPY: a 10-week-old German Shepherd puppy, black-and-tan, one ear up and one ear floppy, teal collar, oversized paws, big innocent brown eyes.

Shot 5 (15–18s): Low, ground-level shot inside a bright, sunlit living room with sunlight streaming through the window onto a red Persian rug. The German Shepherd puppy chases a small colorful rubber ball, then stops, sits, and tilts its head toward the door, floppy ear flipping, and gives a tiny squeaky yip. Dust particles float in the sunbeam.

Shot 6 (18–21s): Extreme close-up of the Owner's face in the doorway. He slowly sets the chai cup down on a side table without looking away. His jaw tightens and his eyes go ice-cold. He says quietly, with menace: "ग़लत कुत्ता चुना है तुमने।" (You picked the wrong dog.) Deep bass hit, silence.

Shot 7 (21–24s): Wide exterior shot from the street, sunny day. A beat of stillness, then the Angel of Death is flung backward out of the front door in slow motion, flying over the porch steps, robe flapping, and lands flat on his back on the green lawn. His scythe spins through the air and sticks into the grass beside him. Startled birds fly off a tree. Sound: whoosh, crash, heavy thud.

Shot 8 (24–27s): The Owner walks calmly down the porch steps and stands over the Angel of Death. The German Shepherd puppy trots out beside his feet in the sunshine, puffs out its tiny chest and gives one small, brave "Woof!" The Angel slowly raises a bony finger to argue, looks at the man, then at the puppy, then lowers the finger and says wearily: "ठीक है… आज नहीं।" (Fine… not today.)

Shot 9 (27–30s): The Owner scoops up the puppy and sits on the sunny porch steps with it. The puppy happily licks his chin, tail wagging. He finally gives a small half-smile and scratches behind its floppy ear. In the background, the Angel of Death gets up, picks up his scythe and limps away out of the garden gate. Warm golden sunlight, gentle breeze, birds chirping again. On-screen text: "आज नहीं। ग़लत कुत्ता। 🐾" (Not today. Wrong dog.)

Style: photorealistic, natural motion, realistic fur and sunlight, accurate German Shepherd puppy anatomy, cinematic color grade, funny and heartwarming tone. No cartoon look, no distorted hands, no extra fingers or legs, no watermark.
