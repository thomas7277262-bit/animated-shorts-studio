Every time I sneeze, I swap places with a random stranger. And they land at my school desk. And today, the pollen count was the highest in history. My name is Max. It started in the middle of a math test. Achoo! Suddenly I was in a pizza shop, with dough flying over my head. And the pizza chef? He was sitting at my desk, staring at my math test. Achoo! Now I was a goalkeeper, in a giant stadium, with fifty thousand people screaming. The ball flew straight at my face. I sneezed again. Achoo! I was in the zoo. Inside the lion cage. Holding a bucket of meat. The lion looked at me. I looked at the lion. The zookeeper was now in my math class. Achoo! A wedding. Everyone was staring at me. I was holding the ring. Achoo! A fire truck. And I was driving it. I'm eleven! Then came the biggest sneeze of my life. I opened my eyes in the cockpit of a plane, high above the clouds. And the pilot? He was in my classroom, doing long division. The plane started to dive. Alarms everywhere. I needed one more sneeze. But nothing came. I searched everywhere. Then I found it. The pilot's lunch. A pepper sandwich. I took one big sniff. Achoo! The pilot was back in the sky. And I was back at my desk. My whole class was clapping. The teacher handed back my test. A plus. Perfect score. Because a chef, a goalkeeper, a zookeeper, and a pilot had each solved one question. The doctor says I'll grow out of it. But tomorrow is the math final. So tonight, I'm buying pepper.
