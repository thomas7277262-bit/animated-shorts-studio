In this town, every pet has a power level floating above its head. Lions get nine thousand. Dragons get fifty thousand. And my hamster, Nugget? Zero. Literally zero. The bully, Brax, had a tiger with twelve thousand. On the first day of school, it knocked Nugget across the playground, and the whole school filmed it. I picked him up, and he was shaking. But that night, I noticed something weird. His number… had moved. Zero… to one. The next morning, Nugget pushed a lost kitten out of the road. Seven. He carried my homework through the rain. Forty. He even shared his last sunflower seed with a hungry pigeon. Ninety. That's when I figured it out. Nugget didn't get stronger by fighting. He got stronger by protecting people. So we trained. Every single day. We helped Grandma carry her groceries. Three hundred. We saved a little kid's balloon from a tree. Two thousand. We carried a lost puppy all the way home. Ten thousand. We stopped a runaway shopping cart. Eighty-eight thousand! But Brax just laughed. "A hamster with eighty-eight thousand? Prove it." Then the sky turned purple. A shadow monster rose over the city, taller than every skyscraper. And everywhere it walked, every pet's power dropped to zero. The lions. The dragons. Even Brax's tiger. Police dogs, fire dragons, even the mayor's giant eagle. Nothing worked. The monster turned toward the school. Toward Brax. And nobody could stop it. Nobody… except Nugget. He stood in front of the whole school, and the monster laughed. I yelled, "Nugget, run!" But he didn't run. He looked back at me… and smiled. Then Nugget's number started glitching. Nine… nine, nine… nine billion! And he grew. Bigger than the trees. Bigger than the school. A giant golden hamster, glowing like the sun. One punch, and the monster exploded into stars. The whole city chanted his name. Nugget! Nugget! Every pet got its power back. And Brax walked over, and for the first time ever… he said sorry. Nugget shrank back down, curled up in my hand, and fell asleep. But then I looked at Brax's tiger. And its number… was glitching too.
