In my town, there's one rule. Never open the door after midnight.
Grandma taught me the secret. Real people knock three times. It always knocks four.
Last Friday, I was home alone.
At exactly midnight… knock.
"Ben? It's Mom. I forgot my keys."
It was her voice. Her exact voice.
I reached for the lock. And I started counting.
One. Two. Three.
Four.
I froze.
"Ben, open the door. It's so cold out here."
Then my phone buzzed. It was Mom.
"Hi sweetie, we're staying at Grandma's tonight."
I looked at the door. The knocking had stopped.
Then I heard it again. Knock. Knock. Knock. Knock.
But this time… it was coming from inside my closet.
